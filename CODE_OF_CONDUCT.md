# Code of Conduct

Track Maker follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

- Be welcoming and respectful to everyone, whatever their experience with CAD,
  3D printing or code.
- Give and take feedback on ideas and designs, not on people.
- No harassment, insults, personal attacks, or sharing other people's private
  information.

## Scope

This applies in the issues, pull requests and discussions of this repository,
and anywhere someone represents the project.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer, [@drjonesy](https://github.com/drjonesy),
through GitHub. Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may edit or remove
comments, issues and contributions, and may block anyone who breaks this code,
following the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
